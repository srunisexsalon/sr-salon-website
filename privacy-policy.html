Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how SR Unisex Salon handles information when you visit our website, contact us or use our salon services.

Last Updated: September 7, 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy

SR Unisex Salon respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy describes the general ways in which information may be collected, used and protected when you access or interact with the SR Unisex Salon website.

By using this website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the website.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website or salon, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide to us or information that is automatically collected when you visit the website.

Information You Provide

You may choose to provide information when you contact us, request an appointment, communicate through WhatsApp, call the salon or make an enquiry about our services.

This information may include your name, phone number, appointment details, service preferences and any other information you voluntarily provide during your communication with us.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically. This may include information such as browser type, device type, approximate location, pages visited, referring pages, date and time of access and general website usage information.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used for legitimate business and customer-service purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and customer questions.
  • Managing appointment requests and bookings.
  • Providing information about salon services.
  • Improving our website and customer experience.
  • Understanding general website usage and performance.
  • Communicating with customers regarding their requested services or appointments.

3. WhatsApp, Phone and Other Communications

Our website may provide links that allow visitors to contact SR Unisex Salon by phone or WhatsApp. When you choose to use these communication methods, the information you provide is handled through the respective communication platform as well as by our salon for the purpose of responding to your enquiry.

We recommend that you avoid sending unnecessary sensitive or confidential information through public or messaging platforms.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand visitor behaviour and improve the overall user experience.

Cookies are small data files stored on your device by websites. Depending on your browser settings, you may be able to block or delete cookies. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

5. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. This can help us understand general traffic patterns, popular pages and website performance.

Analytics information is generally used in an aggregated manner to improve our website and online services. Google may process information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

6. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may contain links to third-party services such as Google Maps, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp or other external websites and platforms.

These third-party websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and terms of use. SR Unisex Salon is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

7. Google Maps

Our website may display or link to Google Maps to help customers find SR Unisex Salon in Gaya. When you interact with Google Maps, Google may collect and process information according to Google's applicable policies.

8. How We Protect Information

We take reasonable measures to protect information provided to us from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted through the internet or third-party communication services.

9. Information Sharing

SR Unisex Salon does not intend to sell or rent customer information to third parties.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to provide a requested service, operate our website, communicate with you, comply with applicable law or protect the rights and safety of our business, customers or others.

10. Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including appointment management, customer service, business records, legal requirements and legitimate operational needs.

The actual retention period may vary depending on the type and purpose of the information.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children for purposes that are not appropriate under applicable law.

12. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide certain information. However, this may prevent us from responding to an enquiry, arranging an appointment or providing certain requested services.

You may also manage cookies through your browser settings and may choose how you interact with third-party services linked from our website.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, business practices, technology or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.
