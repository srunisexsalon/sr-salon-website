Nail Extension Care & Maintenance

Nail extensions need regular care and maintenance. Avoid using the extensions as tools for opening, scraping or picking things, and do not pull or remove them forcefully. If an extension lifts, it is better to have it checked or removed professionally rather than trying to tear it off.

If your natural nail is painful, swollen, visibly irritated or shows signs of infection, postpone the cosmetic service and seek appropriate professional advice. Nail extensions are a beauty service and do not treat nail infections or medical nail conditions.
