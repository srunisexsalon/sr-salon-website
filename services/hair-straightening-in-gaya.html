Not All Hair Straightens the Same Way

Thick, curly hair usually takes more time and careful handling than fine or wavy hair. If your hair's already been through other chemical treatments, that matters too — we're not going to layer chemical process on chemical process without checking first.

Some people want it dead straight. Others just want less frizz and easier mornings. Tell us which one you're after — it changes the whole approach.

What the Appointment Looks Like

Consultation first, then prep, then the actual straightening process worked through in sections, finished carefully. How long it takes depends on your hair's length and thickness — long, dense hair naturally takes longer than short or fine hair.

After the Treatment

Chemically treated hair needs a bit of extra care — right products, less heat styling than usual, gentle handling. We'll walk you through what to do afterward based on what treatment you got.

If your hair starts acting unusual afterward — excessive dryness, breakage, anything off — that's worth getting checked out rather than just adding more chemical treatments on top.

Depends on your hair's length, thickness, and condition — there's no single flat rate that makes sense for everyone. We'll give you an actual number once we've had a look at your hair, not a generic quote.
