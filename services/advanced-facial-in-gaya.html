Is an Advanced Facial a Medical Treatment?

An advanced facial is a cosmetic salon service. It may help cleanse, hydrate and refresh the skin's appearance, but it should not be considered a medical or dermatological treatment.

If you have a specific skin condition, allergy or ongoing dermatological concern, consider consulting a dermatologist before booking a facial treatment.
