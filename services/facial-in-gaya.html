Facial in Gaya for Fresh and Well-Cared-For Skin

Sometimes the best thing you can do for your skin is just sit back for an hour and let someone else handle it. That's basically what a facial is — cleansing, exfoliation, hydration, maybe a massage, depending on what you pick and what your skin's asking for.

No two people's skin is the same. Some of us deal with oiliness, some with dryness, some with random sensitivity that shows up out of nowhere. So before anything starts, we ask — what's going on with your skin lately, what are you hoping to get out of this.

More Than Just "Putting Stuff on Your Face"

Cleansing, exfoliation, steam, massage, serum, a mask — a proper facial can involve all of that or just some of it, depending on what you go for. We're not going to push every step on you just to make the service look bigger.

Got something specific in mind already? Tell us. Not sure what you want? We'll walk you through the options instead of just handing you a price list.

Your Skin Type Actually Matters Here

Oily skin and dry skin don't respond the same way to the same treatment. Sensitive skin needs its own kind of care too. We factor that in before picking products or steps — it's not a one-size-fits-all thing here.

If your skin's been acting up, feeling tight, oily, dull, whatever — say so. And if you know you're sensitive to certain things, mention that upfront too.

Doesn't Have to Be for a Special Occasion

Plenty of people come in just because it's been a while, not because there's a wedding coming up. A facial can fit into your regular routine the same way a haircut does — no event required.

How often you come in is really up to you and your skin. There's no universal schedule everyone should follow.

Before a Wedding or Event

That said, a lot of people do book facials ahead of weddings, festivals, parties. If that's you, book early — we can pair it with cleanup, D-Tan, or whatever else you're already planning for the occasion.

Facial vs Cleanup — Not the Same Thing

Cleanup is the quicker, simpler option. A facial goes further — more steps, more time. Neither is "better," it just depends what you're after that day. Ask us if you're stuck between the two.

What to Mention Before We Start

Any known sensitivity, recent irritation, or if you've just had another cosmetic treatment — tell us. It changes how we approach things and keeps the whole session more comfortable for you.

Worth being upfront: this is cosmetic care. If you've got severe or persistent acne, an active infection, or something ongoing, that's really a dermatologist's call, not ours.
