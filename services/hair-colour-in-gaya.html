Choosing the Right Hair Colour

Choosing a hair colour is a personal decision. Some people prefer shades that look close to their natural hair, while others enjoy warmer, cooler or more expressive colours. Your skin tone, existing hair colour and preferred level of maintenance can all be considered when selecting a shade.

It is also important to consider your hair's current condition. Hair that has already been exposed to multiple chemical services may need a different approach from untreated hair. Discussing your previous treatments with your stylist helps create a more suitable colour plan.

Hair Colour Aftercare

Coloured hair can benefit from a consistent hair-care routine. Suitable shampoo, conditioning and careful heat styling can help maintain the overall appearance of coloured hair. The right aftercare routine can vary depending on the colour service and your individual hair condition.

Regular salon maintenance may also be useful when you want to keep your colour looking consistent as your natural roots grow. If you are unsure which products or maintenance routine to follow, you can ask our team for basic aftercare guidance during your appointment.
