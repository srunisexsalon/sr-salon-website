What Is Permanent & Semi-Permanent Makeup?

Permanent and semi-permanent makeup are cosmetic pigmentation techniques used to add definition or colour to selected facial features. Depending on the service, this may include eyebrows, lips or eyeliner. Semi-permanent cosmetic pigmentation generally fades gradually, while permanent makeup is intended to have a longer-lasting appearance. The exact service, technique and expected maintenance can vary, so a consultation is recommended before proceeding.

KNOW THE DIFFERENCE

Permanent vs Semi-Permanent Makeup

The terms permanent and semi-permanent makeup are sometimes used interchangeably, but they can refer to different approaches to cosmetic pigmentation. Understanding the difference can help you discuss the right option during consultation.

Semi-Permanent Makeup

Semi-permanent makeup uses cosmetic pigmentation techniques intended to gradually fade over time. It is often chosen by clients who want a defined but adjustable-looking cosmetic result.

Depending on the service, semi-permanent options may include brows, lip colour or eyeliner. The appearance and longevity can vary according to the technique, skin characteristics, lifestyle and aftercare.

Permanent Makeup

Permanent makeup refers to cosmetic pigmentation designed to provide a longer-lasting appearance. It can be considered for selected facial features where a more lasting cosmetic definition is preferred.

Because the result is intended to last longer than everyday makeup, choosing the design, colour and style carefully is an important part of the consultation process.
